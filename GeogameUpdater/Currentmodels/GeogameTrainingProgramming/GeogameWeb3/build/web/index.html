Welcome to GeoGame!

Grading

Your solution will be graded primarily based on how much money you have spent.

Money is spent by choosing pipes (different price per pipe type) and making Q and T corrections.

Gameplay

Always start above the pumping well. To place a pipe, click on an empty square next to an already existing pipe.

When you click on a square, a dropdown list of pipes will appear. Choose your pipe and place it on the square.

When needed, type the Q of the pipeline in the white pop-up box.

Mistakes on T and Q cost a lot. If you go broke it is GAME OVER!

Watch the top-right of the game screen for information about houses.

Tips

You can RIGHT CLICK on a placed pipe to see its pipeline and T value.

All your Q and T information for each pipeline is displayed to the right of the grid.
